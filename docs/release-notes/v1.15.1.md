# v1.15.1 — The Forge Identity

## Summary

v1.15 rebuilt the structure; this release gives the product a visual identity
that could not be mistaken for anyone else's. The design brief came from the
Anthropic frontend-design discipline: ground the tokens in the subject's own
world, spend boldness in exactly one place, and audit the result with an
independent eye rather than grading your own homework.

## The de-templating

The v1.15 palette was the documented default look AI-generated products
cluster into: near-black surfaces plus one acid cyan accent — legible as
"dev tool template", anonymous as a signature. The forge has its own palette:

| Token | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Forge charcoal | warm-neutral surfaces, never blue-black | the shop floor |
| **Ember orange** | `#E8A33D` | the working accent — the color of governed heat |
| Copper | `#D96C3F` | secondary warmth |
| Hot-metal ivory | `#F2E9DC` | decisive text |
| Botanical green / forge red | pass/fail | instant learned recognition |

The light theme is calibrated for the same identity on day-shift steel — not
an inversion.

## The signature: the heat-thread

One aesthetic risk, spent once. A thin ember gradient appears only where work
is LIVE or a decision is being asked of you — the live terminal badge, the
approval gate. Cool surfaces stay cool: heat means activity, and the
interface never fakes heat. The brand mark gains an ember core; section marks
use a quiet ember rule; the intake headline is a steel-to-ember gradient.

## Audit-driven, not self-graded

Thirteen captures across every surface in both themes, graded by an
independent vision model (GLM-5V):

- The number-one repeated finding — a dead void in the center of idle/failed
  runs — is fixed: **a failed run is now an incident card** (what happened,
  the preserved trail, an Inspect-the-trail action), and **empty states are
  directional invitations** instead of apologies.
- Light-mode headline weight, sidebar status contrast, and failed-badge
  contrast were tuned to the audit's specifics.
- Post-fix re-audit: intake-light **9/10**, activity **9/10**, failed-run
  workbench **8/10** — from 6.0–7.5 before the fixes.
